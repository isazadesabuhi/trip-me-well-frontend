## Top Attractions in Marseille

1. **Vieux-Port (Old Port)**  
    The heart of Marseille, the Old Port is lined with cafes, restaurants, and bars. Enjoy fresh seafood or take a boat ride to the nearby Frioul Islands.
   <img src="https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2020/03/20/08/47/marseille-4950003_960_720.jpg" alt="Vieux-Port (Old Port)" />

2. **Notre-Dame de la Garde**  
   A stunning basilica perched on a hill, offering panoramic views of the city and coastline. The golden statue of the Virgin Mary is an iconic symbol of Marseille.

3. **Le Panier**  
   Marseille’s oldest neighborhood, known for its narrow streets, colorful buildings, and street art. Visit artisan shops and enjoy its bohemian vibe.

4. **Calanques National Park**  
    A natural paradise of limestone cliffs, turquoise waters, and hidden beaches. Ideal for hiking, swimming, and kayaking.
   <img src="https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2017/01/09/16/15/calanque-1966920_960_720.jpg" alt="Calanques National Park" />

5. **MuCEM (Museum of European and Mediterranean Civilizations)**  
   A striking modern museum showcasing Mediterranean culture and history, located near the Old Port.

6. **Château d’If**  
   Famous as the setting of _The Count of Monte Cristo_, this fortress-turned-prison is a short boat ride from the Old Port.

7. **Corniche Kennedy**  
   A scenic coastal road offering breathtaking views of the Mediterranean. Perfect for a leisurely walk or drive.

8. **Plages du Prado (Prado Beaches)**  
   Marseille’s popular beach area, great for swimming, sunbathing, or picnics.

9. **La Canebière**  
   Marseille’s historic main street, lined with shops, cafes, and historic buildings.

10. **Cité Radieuse (Le Corbusier)**  
    A UNESCO-listed modernist building designed by architect Le Corbusier, featuring apartments, a rooftop terrace, and an art gallery.


## What to Do in Marseille: Itinerary Guides

### 1 Day in Marseille

- **Morning**: Start at the Old Port and take a boat to Château d’If.
- **Afternoon**: Visit Le Panier and enjoy lunch at a traditional seafood restaurant.
- **Evening**: Head to Notre-Dame de la Garde for sunset views and dine along the Corniche Kennedy.

### 1 Week in Marseille

- **Day 1**: Explore the Old Port, Château d’If, and MuCEM.
- **Day 2**: Wander through Le Panier and visit La Canebière.
- **Day 3**: Spend the day hiking or kayaking in Calanques National Park.
- **Day 4**: Relax at Prado Beaches and stroll along Corniche Kennedy.
- **Day 5**: Visit Notre-Dame de la Garde and Cité Radieuse.
- **Day 6**: Take a day trip to Cassis, a charming coastal town nearby.
- **Day 7**: Enjoy a local market experience, like the one at Noailles, and savor a bouillabaisse meal.

### 1 Month in Marseille

In addition to the above:

- Explore nearby Provençal towns like Aix-en-Provence and Avignon.
- Dive into Marseille’s art and music scene, including local festivals.
- Take boat tours to lesser-known Calanques or Frioul Islands.
- Join a cooking class to learn how to make bouillabaisse.


## Tips for Visiting Marseille

- **Best Time to Visit**: Spring (April-May) and Fall (September-October) for mild weather and fewer crowds. Summer is great for beach lovers but can get crowded.
- **Transport**: Use the RTM network for buses, trams, and the metro. Consider a City Pass for free public transport and entry to major attractions.
- **Dining**: Don’t miss trying bouillabaisse, Marseille’s famous fish stew, or _navettes_, a traditional orange blossom biscuit.
- **Safety**: Like any big city, be cautious in crowded areas and avoid walking alone in unfamiliar places at night.
